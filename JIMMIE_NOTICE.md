# Notice

## What this is

Jimmie 64 is an unofficial fan project: a skateboarding companion character for Super Mario 64,
built as changes to the community PC port [sm64coopdx](https://github.com/coop-deluxe/sm64coopdx).

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo. Super Mario 64, Mario and related
names are trademarks of Nintendo. This repository contains no ROM, no game code and nothing
extracted from a ROM. To play you need your own legally obtained copy of the game and your own
build of the port; neither is provided here and none will be.

## The person

Jimmie is based on the streamer floppyjimmie and is used with his permission. Three things here are his and are **not** covered by any software
licence in this repository:

- his name and likeness,
- his words: the lines in `brain/jimmie_brain/data` marked as real are sentences he said on stream,
- his voice.

In the game his voice is an **AI voice clone**. Lines marked as real are his own sentences spoken
by the clone; other lines were written by a language model in his style and edited by hand, and he
never said them. The clone's reference recording and the transcripts of his streams are not in
this repository and will not be published. Please do not use anything here to make him appear to
say things outside this project. If he asks for any of this to be taken down, it will be.
